SubTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Overview

SubTrack is a subscription tracking app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

SubTrack stores your subscription data locally on your device. If you create an account, your subscription data is synced to our servers solely to enable cross-device synchronization. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Account Data

  • Email address — used for authentication only
  • Subscription records (names, amounts, renewal dates) — to power the app features
  • Push notification tokens — to deliver renewal reminders

Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics SDKs. RevenueCat processes payment transactions and may collect anonymized purchase data per their own privacy policy.

Offline Mode

SubTrack works fully offline. In offline mode, all data stays on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your local data at any time from Settings → Delete all data. To request deletion of server-side data, contact us at skorukalex03@gmail.com.

iCloud Backup

If you use the iCloud backup feature, your subscription data is stored in your personal iCloud account. We do not have access to this data.
